Tested: `claude/local-takeover` at d0e9020 (+ the QA tooling commit). Browser only (dev stage,
Vite SSR, Playwright on Edge), no Rust build. The animation agent is reworking idle and the
cursor bite in its worktree right now: everything marked **[may change]** below touches idle or
bite and should be re-checked after that lands.

careful eye), **1** = polish / only reachable through debug triggers / data hygiene.

All stage runs use `seed=11`. Timestamps in the repro lines are the stage page's fake-clock
milliseconds as printed on the strips.

What was clean (checked, nothing found):

- No semi-transparent pixels anywhere, no grey/white halo at 1x or 2x on dark or light
  backgrounds (`halo`/`halo1x` = 0 for all 504 frames), outlines closed on every character frame.
- Baselines: every grounded frame has its feet on row 89 (the common line); character height is
  consistent between families (idle 55, walk 54, sit 46-47, the stand-up clips 53-54 at their
  ends), so no size jumps between families.
- No unknown frame names in any animation, no frame shown for a single tick (<= 60 ms) between
  two of the same, no `flip` key on a frame that never mirrors.
- Walk/run/climb/struggle/dance/typing/listen loops: no seam pop, no body jitter over 1 art px.
- Window moving slowly under his feet: feet stay exactly on the window top (gap -1 px for the
  whole 4 s drag). Window closed under him: falls, lands on the next window, recovers.
- Annoyance ladder works as designed: pick-up 1 dangle, 2 annoyed after landing, 3 struggle +
  cling to cursor + annoyed, 4+ struggle + cling + bite + sulk, recovery with calmDown.
- Held: scruff anchor stays under the cursor while held still, while shaken at 10 Hz and while
  dragged slowly; heldKick kicks in when shaken; dragging to all four corners, above the top and
  onto the taskbar never loses him (window clamps to about half off-screen, he falls back on release).
- Chat panel / moods: thinking (25 s, with the laptop typing variant inside the think loop),
  talking, listening, looking (= listen, by design), asking, happy, talk(120 chars) all loop
  without pops; panel open for 10 s keeps him still and turned towards the panel.
- Movement off mid-walk: stops into idle through the drawn turn, no slide.

## Issues

### Severity 3

**S3-1. Clicking him on a wall or the ceiling plays the front-facing "startled" rotated with the surface (upside down on the ceiling).**
Repro: `node dev/vqa-stage.mjs clickDuring` (seed 11): `teleport` lands him on the ceiling
(`cling`), click at t=111 432 ms. Strip `dev/out/vqa/stage/ref-clickDuring-016-click-during-teleport.png`,
findings `cut-clickDuring-5.png` .. `cut-clickDuring-7.png` (climb0 -> surprised0 -> surprised7 -> climb0,
family side->front->side, unmasked). `restAnim()` and `playAction()` both refuse front poses off the
floor ("a front-facing emote would look like waving lying down"), but `pointerUp()` in
`src/mascot/creature.ts` plays `startled` whenever `mode === "stand"`.
Fix: in `pointerUp`, when `!isStanding(this.surface)` play a wall-safe flinch instead, e.g.
`this.animator.interject(() => [k("climb0", 60, {glitch: .5, fx: "eye"}), k("climb7", 220), k("climb0", 120)])`
(the LOOK_BACK frames), or simply a `glitchBurst(300)`.

**S3-2. Rapid clicking makes him vibrate between two frames and never escalates.**
Repro: `clicks` scenario, 30 clicks at 10/s from t=6 250 ms: `startled` restarts on every click,
so for 3 s he alternates surprised0/surprised1 (head bobbing 5 px at 10 Hz).
`ref-clicks-003-rapid-click-mid.png`, `ref-clicks-004-rapid-click-end.png`. Annoyance reaches 12.8
(HIGH is 4.5) from clicks alone, but clicking never shows annoyance: only a landing does
(`reactAfterLanding`). Likely to be the first thing a new user does. **[may change: bite]**
Fix: in `pointerUp`, if `startled` is already playing and started < 500 ms ago, do not restart it
(interject a 2-key glitch flinch instead); once `annoyance >= ANNOY_MEDIUM` play `annoyed`, and at
`ANNOY_HIGH` the grumpy -> sulk path (set `sulkUntil` like `restAnim` does).

### Severity 2

**S2-1. Sheet-slicing bleed: a dark dash on the top row of 11 frames.**
`dangle4-7`, `spin1-4`, `glide2`, `fall_flail4-5` have 3-5 opaque pixels in row 0 that belong to
the cell above (dash 4.5-7.5 CSS px wide, 40-60 px above his head). fall_flail is played on every
drop and throw, dangle4-7 while held (heldKick). Zooms: `dev/out/vqa/zoom/z-topclip.png`,
`z-grab-glide.png`; listed in `dev/out/vqa/frames.txt` (`clip:top`).
Fix: in `scripts/pack-sprites.py` (or the slicer) clear connected components that touch the top
edge and are < 8 px, then re-pack; a unit test "no opaque pixel in row 0 except components
connected to the body" would keep it fixed.

**S2-2. Clicking while he sits pops straight from sit to a standing flinch.**
Repro: `clickDuring`, `sit` then click at t=31 782 ms: sit0 -> surprised0 in one frame, no
stand-up. `dev/out/vqa/stage/cut-clickDuring-1.png`, `ref-clickDuring-002-click-during-sit.png`.
`startled` is in `NO_BRIDGE`. Same for napRock and sitEdge.
Fix: when the shown frame is in the sit family, lead `startled` with `stand_up_hop` (a jump up,
fits a fright) or a `glitchCut`; keep NO_BRIDGE for the air/held cases.

**S2-3. Clicking a sleeping Glitch skips the wake-up.**
Repro: `clickDuring`, `sleep`, then the cursor moves onto him and clicks (t=45 332 ms): the hover
wakes him into the get_up bridge, the click 150 ms later replaces it with `startled`, so
get_up0 (lying curled) cuts to surprised0 (standing) in one frame
(`ref-clickDuring-004-click-during-sleep.png`). The same happens when the click itself wakes him.
Fix: in `pointerUp`, if he was asleep within the last ~1 s (or a curled/get_up/wake frame is
showing), let the wake-up finish (optionally with a glitch burst) instead of `startled`.

**S2-4. The bite draws a second cursor.** **[may change: bite]**
`bite_cursor0-5` have a white cursor arrow drawn into the art. In the annoyance run he bites it
right after landing while the real cursor is 100+ px away (`ref-annoyance-011-pickup-4-after.png`,
t=39 185 ms), so the user sees two arrows. bite_cursor5 -> turn_to_back0 is also a cut (head jumps
13 art px, `dev/out/vqa/switch/11-bite_cursor5-turn_to_back0.png`).
Fix: only play the bite when the real cursor is within ~40 px of his mouth (otherwise `annoyed`
then turn_to_back); better, erase the arrow from the art and draw the `cursor` prop at the real
cursor position. End the bite on an in-between (turn_front_to_side0-2) before turn_to_back.

**S2-5. grabCursor turns side-on without a turn.**
`surprised2` (front) -> `jump0` (side) is a direct cut in `grabCursorKeys` (unmasked, head jumps
16 art px): `dev/out/vqa/switch/solo-grabCursor-surprised2-jump0.png`.
Fix: insert `...toSide(mem)` (or turn_front_to_side2-5) between them.

**S2-6. Getting up from sitting on a window edge pops twice.**
`edgeStandUp`: sit_edge_swing7 (legs hanging, 77 art px tall) -> sit_down7 (47 px) in one frame,
then sit_down7 -> stand_up_hop0. Seen in 72 switch pairs (every way out of sitEdge):
`dev/out/vqa/switch/03-sit_edge_swing7-sit_down7.png`, `04-sit_down7-stand_up_hop0.png`.
Fix: lead the slide with `sit_edge_swing` played back to frame 0 and a 2-key glitch (0.4) on the
first sit_down7 key, and use stand_up_paws (it starts from a matching crouch) instead of the hop
for this path.

**S2-7. "build" ends mid-jump.**
`buildKeys`: point5 -> celebrate2 (head 10 px), then celebrate4 (arms up, mid-hop, 67 px tall) ->
idle0 for 200 ms. `dev/out/vqa/switch/solo-build-celebrate4-idle0.png`,
`solo-build-point5-celebrate2.png`; 119 switch pairs end the same way.
Fix: `...cycle("celebrate", [2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7], 110)` (5-7 settle back down), and start from
celebrate0-1 or bridge point5 with a 60 ms glitch key.

**S2-8. Stretch fidget: blue eye.** **[may change: idle]**
`stretch5` has the glitch eye drawn cyan/blue instead of magenta (`dev/out/vqa/zoom/z-misc.png`).
Shown for 130 ms in a common idle fidget (weight 1.5).
Fix: recolour the eye pixels of stretch5 to the magenta ramp of stretch4/6 in the source sheet.

**S2-9. Window yanked fast: 1-tick idle flash between two falls.**
Repro: `world`, he stands on the Browser window, it moves 300 px in 300 ms (t=40 300 ms):
startled (2 ticks) -> fall_flail -> idle0 for one 50 ms tick -> fall_flail.
`dev/out/vqa/stage/cut-world-1.png`, `ref-world-002-window-fast-move.png`. Event log: `slip`,
`ledge-gone:slip`, `land:ledge`, `ledge-gone:slip` within 200 ms.
Fix: after a slip, ignore a landing on the same ledge id for ~250 ms (or require the contact to
persist for 2 polls before switching to the rest animation).

**S2-10. Interrupting the edge peek snaps him upright.**
`peekEdgeKeys` leans 27 degrees with dx -20; any interruption (click, chat, mood) cuts to an
upright frame 20 px away in one frame. `dev/out/vqa/switch/21-turn_front_to_side5-turn_side_to_front0.png`
(29 pairs). Fix: give peekEdge an `outro` that eases rot/dx back to 0 in 2-3 keys (like
`edgeStandUp`), or make the Animator interpolate dx/rot from the last pose for the first key
of a bridge.

**S2-11. Lying down shows his back for a frame.** **[may change: idle]**
lie_down2 -> lie_down3 (a back view) -> lie_down4 (curled, facing you): two big cuts every time he
falls asleep (91 pairs). `dev/out/vqa/switch/01-lie_down3-lie_down4.png`,
`02-lie_down2-lie_down3.png`. It reads as a turn-around, but at 120 ms per frame it flashes.
Fix: hold lie_down3 for 200 ms with ease, or skip frame 3 (pick [0,1,2,4,5,6,7]).

**S2-12. Interrupting a clip mid-way cuts from wherever it was.**
E.g. the wake clip stopped at wake4 (arms up, tail up) by any new animation: wake4 -> sit_down0,
-> turn_front_to_side0, -> turn_to_back0, -> dangle0, -> climb0 (`dev/out/vqa/switch/35-*`, `36-*`,
`39-*` .. `41-*`). The bridge only looks at the family, and clip frames in the second half
count as the end family.
Fix: when interrupting a running clip (sit_down, stand_up_*, wake, get_up, lie_down, turn_*),
finish it at double speed before the bridge (they are 6-8 frames, < 400 ms at 2x), or play it
backwards to its start.

**S2-13. The cursor bite path and the cling path repeat identically.** **[may change: bite]**
Pick-ups 4 to 8 in a row give exactly the same 5.5 s sequence (struggle 1.8 s -> clingCursor 1.35 s
-> fall -> bite -> turn_to_back -> sulk). `dev/out/vqa/stage/report-annoyance.json`,
`ref-annoyance-013..024`. Fix: vary (skip the cling sometimes, sulk sitting with the back turned,
a glitch-out escape at very high annoyance).

## Coverage and what is still open

Done: frame audit (all 504 frames), animation audit (70 animations x 3 seeds), switch matrix
(4830 pairs), live: clicks, click during 16 activities, hold/drag/edges, annoyance ladder,
long moods, settings (movement, chaos toggle, panel), window moves/close/stack.

Not testable in the stage: chaos mischief (no director without Rust; `setChaos` toggles were
exercised, `chaos` act only spins), work-area/resolution change and other taskbar edges (the
stage's work area is fixed), voice input itself (only the `listening` mood).

## Results of the remaining live scenarios

- **Throws** (`report-throws-part1.json`, `report-throws.json`, `ref-throws-*`): gentle -> flail
  -> land -> idle; hard -> tumble -> splat -> dizzy -> idle; upward -> tumble -> splat on a window
  top -> dizzy; sideways -> tumble -> annoyed; into the right wall -> bounces off and tumbles back
  across the screen -> flail -> land; dropped from just under the top -> flail -> lands on a window;
  tossed onto the Terminal -> lands on its top. No unmasked pops; the splat land3 -> land4 cut is
  hidden by the glitch (masked). Note: a flick of ~75 px per 16 ms (a normal "throw") already
  reaches the screen wall and bounces, so most throws end up on the other side of the screen.
- **Chat in every state** (`report-chatStates.json`): walking, sitting, asleep (wakes, thinks,
  talks), on a window top: all go think -> talk -> idle with bridges. On the right wall: stays
  `cling` (S1-19). While held: stays held, thinks after landing. Airborne: lands, `annoyed` (he
  was thrown), then the mood. Closing mid-reply: back to idle at once, no pop.
- **Emotes on surfaces** (`report-emotesOnSurfaces.json`): all 14 emotes play on the floor and on
  a window top; wave from sitting goes through stand-up first. On the wall/ceiling the
  `playAction` guard refuses front emotes (only the click path leaks, S3-1).
- **Long idle** (`report-idleLong.json`, movement off): falls asleep after 10 min (600 s) through
  yawn -> lie_down -> sleep; hover wakes him with the drawn wake clip (50 ticks, clean); a click
  wakes him into `startled` (S2-3); picking him up asleep goes straight to `held` (fine).
  The `family`/`blip` findings in this run are artefacts of the coarse sampling (5 s skipped
  between 1 s samples) and were discarded after checking the strips.
- **Roaming, 20 simulated minutes** (`report-roam.json`, seed 11): 26 plans (jump 6, stroll 5,
  lookAround 4, hopDown 3, malfunction 2, build, hangOn, slideDown, copter, peekEdge, sleep 1
  each); no plan more than twice in a row, no 3-plan sequence repeated. Idle fidgets seen: turn
  (glance) 29, groom 4, look_back 4, sneeze 2, scratch 2, sit 1, shake_off 1, stretch 1.
  2407 repaints in 20 min (2/s). Nothing repeats identically too often. **[may change: idle]**
- **Click during each activity** (`ref-clickDuring-*`): every activity is interrupted by
  `startled` (S1-11); airborne clicks are ignored (catching needs a drag); see S2-2, S2-3, S3-1.

does not see. The issues above are the ones confirmed by eye on the strips.
